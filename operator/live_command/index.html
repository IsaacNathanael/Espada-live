i Safety

CLAIM BOUNDARY

What ESPADA will not claim

  • A dark SAR feature is a slick candidateIt is not automatic confirmation that the material is oil.
  • A vessel score is comparative evidenceIt is not a probability of guilt or proof of discharge.
  • Missing evidence remains missingLive mode does not replace failed or empty providers with synthetic data.
  • Human authority controls consequencesESPADA can shortlist or abstain; it cannot accuse or authorize enforcement.
